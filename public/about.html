ORIGIN & PURPOSE

Why We Started

Born out of a clear understanding of the challenges pilgrims face during planning.

ZILHAJ was founded after observing how difficult it can be for pilgrims to compare services across multiple providers. Pricing is often inconsistent, information is scattered, and customers are frequently unsure which offer is truly the best.

While exploring Hajj & Umrah services, it became clear that pilgrims were spending significant time contacting different providers just to find reliable options.

This led to a simple but powerful idea: instead of pilgrims searching across many providers, providers should compete to offer the best service to the pilgrim.

With this idea, ZILHAJ was created to build a transparent and trusted platform where pilgrims can submit one request, receive multiple verified offers, and make confident decisions.

“Let the pilgrim focus on the journey, not the search.”
